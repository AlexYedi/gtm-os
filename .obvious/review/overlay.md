Read `AGENTS.md` first. This repo is public.

## Blocker (score ≥ 90) if the PR:
- commits `.env*`, anything under a `raw/` research folder, a transcript, a confidential name, or a comp figure
- adds LinkedIn or X scraping
- adds code that writes to Supabase, Notion, HubSpot or Linear
- builds against the retired spine (`abkvgihlbwfloentugtd`, `SUPABASE_SPINE_*`)
- states a number with no measured source
