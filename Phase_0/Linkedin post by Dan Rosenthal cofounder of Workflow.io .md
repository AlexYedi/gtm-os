These are the 11 skills we look for in a complete GTM engineer:

1️⃣ Tech Stack Evaluations  
\- Knowing which GTM tools to allocate budget to.

2️⃣ Workflow Design  
\- Automations across tools like HubSpot, Clay, and Claude Code.

3️⃣ TAM Mapping  
\- Modelling an ICP and building an AI-qualified account list.

4️⃣ Signal Tracking  
\- Activating 1st-, 2nd-, and 3rd-party intent signals.

5️⃣ Data Enrichment  
\- Mining relevant account/contact research to inform the strategy.

6️⃣ Automated Outbound  
\- Automated e-mail and DM campaigns to avoid manual prospecting.

7️⃣ Inbound Orchestration  
\- Designing inbound flows to maximize conversion and stay organized.

8️⃣ Ads Audiences  
\- Creating audiences out of TALs is straightforward with the above skills (you don't need to be a media buying expert).

9️⃣ Scoring Systems  
\- Account-fit and awareness scoring to find the biggest opportunities.

🔟 CRMs  
\- Sales process and technical execution in HubSpot or Salesforce.

... and potentially the most powerful of all.

Content Engineering.
